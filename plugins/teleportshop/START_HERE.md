## What is TeleportShop?

A premium Bukkit/Paper plugin for Minecraft servers providing:
- ✅ **Homes System**: `/home`, `/sethome`, `/delhome`, `/listhomes`
- ✅ **Warps System**: `/warp`, `/setwarp`, `/delwarp`, `/warplist`  
- ✅ **TPA Requests**: `/tpa`, `/tpahere`, `/tpaccept`, `/tpdeny`, `/tpcancel`
- ✅ **Utilities**: `/back`, `/spawn`, `/setspawn`
- ⏳ **Player Shops**: Coming Phase 2
- ⏳ **NPC Traders**: Coming Phase 3
